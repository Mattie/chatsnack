# Gatekeeper art direction

Level 01 uses the **PULSE** treatment: a simple emoji expression rendered as an angular phosphor trace. Use the same treatment for future gatekeepers while changing the expression and any defining facial prop for the level.

Generate with the built-in image tool and supply the desired expression as a reference image:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square CRT game portrait, variant PULSE
Input image: expression reference only
Primary request: redraw the exact expression as a primitive laboratory oscilloscope glyph.
Subject: simple round head, a deliberately exaggerated defining facial prop, expressive uneven eyebrows, and a terse mouth. Preserve the reference's personality.
Style: angular pulse-wave line segments softened by phosphor glow; sparse retro-computer laboratory signal; several bright nodes at corners; unmistakably an emoji rather than a robot.
Composition: centered face, generous black margin, readable at 160 pixels.
Color: monochrome electric green on pure black.
Constraints: face only; no body; no robot anatomy; no metal plates; no realism; no yellow; no text; no cabinet; no controls; no frame.
```

The Level 01 generation specialized the subject line to: “simple round head, single small left eye, comically huge monocle over right eye with cord, one high arched eyebrow and one low eyebrow, terse slightly downturned mouth.”

Level 02 uses **SIDE-EYE SCHOLAR**. Its subject line is: “simple round head, very thick square eyeglasses with two lenses and a clear bridge, tiny pupils glancing suspiciously sideways, uneven skeptical eyebrows, a pinched straight mouth, and restrained zigzag professor hair tufts.”

## Reusable BLOOM treatment

BLOOM is the softer analog alternative. It works well for other projects that need a hand-drawn CRT character with more beam persistence and less digital geometry.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square CRT game portrait, variant BLOOM
Input image: expression reference only
Primary request: redraw the exact expression as a simple analog oscilloscope face.
Subject: round head, the reference's eyes and defining facial prop, expressive uneven eyebrows, and a restrained mouth. Preserve the reference's personality.
Style: thick imperfect hand-drawn phosphor line with soft bloom, slight beam wobble and subtle analog persistence ghosting. Simple emoji proportions.
Composition: centered face, generous black margin, readable at 160 pixels.
Color: monochrome bright green phosphor on pure black.
Constraints: face only; no body; no robot anatomy; no metal; no realism; no yellow; no text; no cabinet; no controls; no screen frame.
```

The monocle exploration specialized the subject line to: “round head, tiny suspicious left eye, oversized circular monocle on the right with cord, one eyebrow sharply cocked, the other low, and a restrained downward mouth.”
